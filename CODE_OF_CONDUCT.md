# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

 ## Preamble

 This Code of Conduct applies to all contributors, maintainers, and participants in the rusty_lucene project community. We aim to create a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

 ## Expected Behavior

 We expect all community members to:

 - **Be welcoming**: Greet newcomers and help them get started
 - **Be inclusive**: Ensure everyone feels included regardless of background
 - **Be respectful**: Treat others with kindness and consideration
 - **Be constructive**: Focus on solutions rather than criticism

 ## Unacceptable Behavior

 Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

 - **Harassment**: Repeated or targeted harassment of any individual
 - **Discrimination**: Discriminatory comments or actions based on identity
 - **Disruption**: Intentionally disrupting meetings, discussions, or development
 - **Violence**: Threats or acts of physical or verbal violence

 ## Enforcement Process

 ### Reporting Mechanism

 If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

 1. **Email**: conduct@rusty-lucene.org
 2. **GitHub Issues**: Use the "Report Conduct Violation" issue template

 Include:
 - Date and time of incident
 - Description of what happened
 - Any relevant context or evidence
 - How you would like to resolve it

 ### Review Timeline

 - Initial response: Within 24 hours
 - Investigation: Within 72 hours
 - Resolution: Within 7 days

 ### Possible Actions

 Based on the investigation, actions may include:

 - Verbal warning
 - Temporary suspension from contribution
 - Permanent removal from project
 - Public or private apology
 - Documentation of the incident

 ### Appeals Process

 If you disagree with a resolution, you may appeal by:

 1. Contacting the enforcement team directly
 2. Providing additional context or evidence
 3. Requesting a review by a different maintainer

 Appeals will be reviewed within 5 business days.

 ## Contact Information

 - **Primary Contact**: conduct@rusty-lucene.org
 - **Alternate Contact**: maintainer@rusty-lucene.org

 ## Enforcement Team

 - Project Maintainers (see CONTRIBUTING.md)
 - Designated Conduct Reviewers

 We commit to responding to all reports in a timely and respectful manner.
